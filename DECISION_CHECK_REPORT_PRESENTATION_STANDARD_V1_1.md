## SECTION-BY-SECTION PRESENTATION STANDARD

### 1. MEASUREMENT

**Purpose**: State the core measurement classification.

**Word Limit**: 3-8 words maximum

**Sentence Structure**: Single statement (no periods, no explanation)

**Format**:
```
Measurement

[Classification Level] Commitment Pressure
```

**Examples**:
```
Measurement
Elevated Commitment Pressure

Measurement
High Commitment Pressure

Measurement
Moderate to Elevated Commitment Pressure
```

**Constraints**:
- ❌ No explanation ("Elevated because...")
- ❌ No technical codes (CPE shown separately in Technical Classification)
- ❌ No context ("Your measurement is...")
- ✅ Classification name only
- ✅ Plain language only
- ✅ Directness required

---

### 2. POSITION

**Purpose**: State customer's placement within the typical range.

**Word Limit**: 1-3 words maximum

**Sentence Structure**: Single phrase

**Format**:
```
Position

[Placement Label]
```

**Valid Placements** (locked):
- Lower Than Typical
- Typical
- Higher Than Typical
- Meaningfully Higher Than Typical

**Examples**:
```
Position
Typical

Position
Higher Than Typical

Position
Meaningfully Higher Than Typical
```

**Constraints**:
- ❌ No explanation
- ❌ No "compared to"
- ❌ No comparison group details
- ✅ Label only
- ✅ Direct placement statement

---

### 3. COMPARED WITH

**Purpose**: Identify the structural peer group.

**Word Limit**: 10-20 words maximum

**Sentence Structure**: Single complete sentence (subject + verb + predicate)

**Format**:
```
Compared With

[Decision Type] supported by [Archetype Translation].
```

**Examples**:
```
Compared With
Home purchases supported by employment income.

Compared With
Retirement decisions supported by recurring client base with project-based income and portfolio assets.

Compared With
Business launches supported by variable, transaction-based income.
```

**Constraints**:
- ❌ No explanation ("because...")
- ❌ No "you are" or "your"
- ❌ No context beyond peer group identity
- ✅ Clean subject-verb-object structure
- ✅ Decision Type + Archetype translation
- ✅ Period ends the statement
- ✅ Maximum 20 words

**Formatting**:
- No bullets
- No dashes
- Single sentence only
- Professional tone

---

### 4. INTERPRETATION

**Purpose**: Explain what the Commitment Pressure measurement means structurally.

**Word Limit**: 30-60 words

**Sentence Structure**: 1-2 complete sentences

**Format**:
```
Interpretation

[Measurement] [decision type] [structural explanation].
```

**Examples**:
```
Interpretation
This home purchase relies meaningfully on the continued strength of the structure supporting it.

Interpretation
This retirement decision depends heavily on sustained transaction completion over a 30-40 year period. The structure cannot withstand extended gaps in transaction flow.

Interpretation
This business launch depends moderately on continued access to client relationships and project opportunities. The supporting structure has some resilience through recurring client base.
```

**Constraints**:
- Minimum: 30 words
- Maximum: 60 words
- 1-2 sentences only
- ❌ No recommendations ("you should")
- ❌ No action plans
- ❌ No predictions ("will" or "likely")
- ✅ Structural description
- ✅ Measurement-specific language
- ✅ No vague language

**Tone**: Objective, deterministic, measured

---

### 5. PRIMARY DRIVERS

**Purpose**: Explain why the customer received this Commitment Pressure classification.

**Word Limit**: Bullets only, 8-12 words per bullet

**Bullet Count**: 3-4 bullets maximum

**Format**:
```
Primary Drivers

• [Structural characteristic relevant to measurement]
• [Structural characteristic relevant to measurement]
• [Structural characteristic relevant to measurement]
```

**Examples**:
```
Primary Drivers

• Support depends on a limited number of sources.
• Support relies on future performance continuing as expected.
• Limited room exists if supporting conditions change.

Primary Drivers

• Income flow depends entirely on continued transaction completion.
• Single primary client concentration creates dependency.
• Long-term horizon (30-40 years) requires sustained transaction flow.
```

**Constraints**:
- 3-4 bullets only (never fewer, never more)
- 8-12 words per bullet
- ❌ No vague language ("various factors")
- ❌ No recommendations
- ❌ No future predictions
- ✅ Specific structural facts
- ✅ Direct causation (why CP is this level)
- ✅ Plain English

**Formatting**:
- Bullet points only
- Complete phrases (not sentence fragments)
- No sub-bullets
- Parallel structure across bullets

---

### 6. IMPLICATIONS

**Purpose**: Explain why the Commitment Pressure matters.

**Word Limit**: 1-2 sentences, 20-35 words total

**Format**:
```
Implications

[Commitment Pressure level] means [what must continue to go right].
```

**Examples**:
```
Implications
Several important things must continue to go right for this home purchase to remain supported.

Implications
Many important things must continue to go right. Extended gaps in transaction flow would threaten the structure.

Implications
Few things must continue to go right for this career change to remain supported.
```

**Constraints**:
- Maximum 2 sentences
- Maximum 35 words total
- ❌ No "you should"
- ❌ No action plans
- ❌ No recommendations
- ✅ Structural consequence statement
- ✅ Direct causation from measurement
- ✅ Plain language

**Tone**: Objective statement of structural dependency

---

### 7. TYPICAL RANGE

**Purpose**: Show what is common in the comparison group.

**Word Limit**: Exactly 1 sentence, 15-25 words

**Format** (Fixed Structure):

```
Typical Range

Most comparable situations fall between [Lower Level] and [Upper Level] Commitment Pressure.
```

**Examples**:
```
Typical Range
Most comparable situations fall between Moderate and Elevated Commitment Pressure.

Typical Range
Most comparable situations fall between Low and Moderate Commitment Pressure.

Typical Range
Most comparable situations fall between Elevated and High Commitment Pressure.
```

**Constraints**:
- Exactly 1 sentence
- Always starts with "Most comparable situations"
- Uses fixed phrasing "fall between X and Y Commitment Pressure"
- ❌ No technical codes (CPM, CPE shown separately)
- ❌ No explanation
- ❌ No "you"
- ✅ Plain language levels only
- ✅ No variability in format

**Formatting**:
- Single sentence only
- Period at end
- No bullets
- No subordinate clauses

---

### 8. TECHNICAL CLASSIFICATION

**Purpose**: Display the classification code for technical reference.

**Format** (Fixed):

```
Technical Classification

[Code]
```

**Codes** (locked):
- CPL
- CPM
- CPE
- CPH
- CPC

**Examples**:
```
Technical Classification

CPE

Technical Classification

CPM – CPE

Technical Classification

CPH
```

**Constraints**:
- Code display only
- No explanation
- No periods
- Optional dash for ranges
- ❌ No text
- ❌ No explanation

## FORMATTING STANDARD

### Headings

**Format**:
- All caps (MEASUREMENT, POSITION, etc.)
- Left-aligned
- No special characters
- Single spacing above
- Single spacing below

**Example**:
```
Measurement

Elevated Commitment Pressure
```

---

### Spacing

**Between Sections**: 1 blank line

**Within Sections**: 0 blank lines (no space between heading and content)

**Example**:
```
Measurement
Elevated Commitment Pressure

Position
Higher Than Typical

Compared With
Home purchases supported by employment income.
```

---

### Paragraph Structure

**General Rule**: No indentation, left-aligned

**Line Length**: 60-80 characters per line (for readability on desktop and mobile)

---

### Bullet Structure (Primary Drivers Only)

**Format**:
- Bullet character: •
- Space after bullet
- Complete phrases, not fragments
- No nested bullets

**Example**:
```
Primary Drivers

• Support depends on a limited number of sources.
• Support relies on future performance continuing as expected.
• Limited room exists if supporting conditions change.
```

---

### Mobile Readability Requirements

- All sections readable on 320px width screens
- No horizontal scrolling required
- Font size minimum: 14px body text
- Line height minimum: 1.5
- Section headings: 18px minimum

---

### Desktop Readability Requirements

- Maximum content width: 600px
- Optimal reading distance: arm's length
- Professional appearance (like Moody's, FICO)
- Sufficient breathing room between sections
