## PART 1: TRANSLATION PRINCIPLE

### The Rule

**Customer understanding comes before framework terminology.**

### Why

Visitors don't care about "Commitment Pressure," "Support Structures," or "gap analysis."

Visitors care about: "Will this decision work if conditions change?"

**Implementation:** Lead with the problem and deliverable. Introduce the measurement name (Commitment Pressure) only after the visitor understands why it matters.

### The Sequence

1. **First 5 seconds**: What problem does this solve? Why now?
2. **Next 5 seconds**: What will I get? What does it show?
3. **Next 20 seconds**: How is this different from what I already know?
4. **Then**: Introduce the system name and how it works.

## PART 7: INTRODUCE COMMITMENT PRESSURE™

### Section Heading
```
Understanding Your Commitment Pressure™ Classification
```

### Copy
```
Every Decision Check™ report includes a Commitment Pressure™ 
classification.

Commitment Pressure™ measures how much your financial decision depends 
on specific conditions continuing to happen.

There are five classifications:

**Low Commitment Pressure**
Few important things must continue to go right. Your decision has 
flexibility to absorb change.

**Moderate Commitment Pressure**
Several things must continue to go right. Your decision requires 
stability in key areas but has some flexibility.

**Elevated Commitment Pressure**
Several important things must continue to go right. Your decision 
requires more stability and has limited flexibility.

**High Commitment Pressure**
Many important things must continue to go right. Your decision is 
dependent on sustained stability across multiple areas.

**Critical Commitment Pressure**
Most key things must continue to go right. Your decision has minimal 
flexibility and depends on nearly everything staying the same.

Your classification shows you where you sit—and what you actually 
depend on.
```

### Why This Works

- Explains the technical term after context established
- Uses plain-language definitions (no jargon in the definitions themselves)
- Shows the full spectrum (Low to Critical)
- Emphasizes what each means practically (flexibility, stability, dependence)
- Uses consistent language ("must continue to go right")

✗ Likelihood of success (no prediction)

✓ Depends on
✓ Must continue
✓ Supported by
✓ Professional assessment
✓ Standardized measurement
✓ Not advice, not prediction
✓ Decision Check™ (product name, always consistent)
✓ Commitment Pressure™ (classification name, introduce late)

✓ Action-oriented (Get, Understand, Know)
✓ Benefit-focused (Your clarity, Your decision)
✓ Specific (Decision Check™ Report)

✗ Fear-based ("Don't miss out")

#### Tone Rules
✓ Professional but accessible
✓ Validating (acknowledges their concern is legitimate)
✓ Clarity-focused (explains, doesn't mystify)
✓ Non-judgmental (shows structure, doesn't judge choice)
✓ Action-oriented (helps them move forward)

✗ Fear-based (avoids anxiety language)
✗ Overly technical (avoids jargon)
