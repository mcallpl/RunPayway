# Customer Evidence Register

**Owner**: Product / Business  
**Effective Date**: Launch  
**Review Frequency**: Weekly (tracking), Monthly (analysis)  
**Status**: LIVE (updated continuously)

---

## Core Principle

**The market decides what RunPayway becomes.**

Not the roadmap. Not the architecture. Not the governance.

The customer evidence.

This register tracks what customers actually do, not what we hope they'll do.

---

## Key Metrics

### ADVISOR METRICS

#### Advisor Signups

**What**: New advisors registering for RunPayway  
**Why**: Growth indicator, customer acquisition  
**Target**: 5/month (by month 3)  
**Tracking**: Weekly count, cumulative

```
Week 1: 0 signups (pre-launch)
Week 2: 2 signups
Week 3: 1 signup
Week 4: 3 signups
Month 1 Total: 6 signups ✅

Month 2: 8 signups
Month 3: 12 signups
Month 4: 15 signups
Trend: Growth ✅
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Advisors find product valuable
- 🔴 Advisors can't find product / don't see value

---

#### Advisor Activation

**What**: Advisors who create at least 1 assessment (% of signups)  
**Why**: Conversion from signup → actual use  
**Target**: >50% (by month 2)  
**Tracking**: Monthly cohort

```
Cohort: Signups in Month 1
  Signups: 6
  Created ≥1 assessment: 4
  Activation Rate: 67% ✅

Cohort: Signups in Month 2
  Signups: 8
  Created ≥1 assessment: 5
  Activation Rate: 63% ✅
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Product is usable, advisors see value
- 🔴 Product is confusing or incomplete, advisors abandon

---

#### Advisor Retention

**What**: % of advisors who continue using product (≥1 assessment per month)  
**Why**: Product stickiness, customer satisfaction  
**Target**: >70% (by month 3)  
**Tracking**: Monthly cohort

```
Cohort: Activated in Month 1 (4 advisors)
  Month 1: 4/4 active (100%)
  Month 2: 3/4 active (75%)
  Month 3: 2/4 active (50%)
  Trend: Declining ⚠️

Cohort: Activated in Month 2 (5 advisors)
  Month 2: 5/5 active (100%)
  Month 3: 4/5 active (80%)
  Trend: Healthy ✅
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Advisors find ongoing value, become regulars
- 🔴 Advisors are one-time users, don't return

---

#### Advisor Engagement (Assessments/Month)

**What**: Average assessments created per active advisor per month  
**Why**: Product usage depth, business model validation  
**Target**: 5 assessments/month per advisor (by month 4)  
**Tracking**: Monthly average

```
Month 1: 1.2 assessments/advisor (early explorers)
Month 2: 2.1 assessments/advisor (getting comfortable)
Month 3: 3.8 assessments/advisor (regular use)
Month 4: 5.2 assessments/advisor ✅

Implication: If growing, advisors find product useful
Implication: Plateau = churn or market saturation
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Product is becoming part of advisor workflow
- 🔴 Product is still a novelty, not integrated

---

### ASSESSMENT METRICS

#### Assessment Volume

**What**: Total assessments completed (cumulative)  
**Why**: Growth indicator, market demand  
**Target**: 50 assessments by month 3, 200+ by month 6  
**Tracking**: Daily count, weekly/monthly sum

```
Month 1: 28 assessments (6 advisors × ~4.7 each)
Month 2: 51 assessments
Month 3: 79 assessments (cumulative: 158)
Month 4: 115 assessments
Month 6 projection: 200+ ✅
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Steady growth = market demand exists
- 🔴 Flat growth = market saturation or churn
- 🔴 Declining = product failing

---

#### Completion Rate (Initiated → Submitted)

**What**: % of assessments started that are actually completed  
**Why**: Product usability, UX friction  
**Target**: >80%  
**Tracking**: Daily/weekly

```
Initiated: 100 assessments
Submitted: 87 assessments
Completion Rate: 87% ✅

(13% abandon during process = UX/friction issue)
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Smooth experience, low friction
- 🔴 Confusing questions, too long, errors in process

---

#### Assessment Errors

**What**: Assessments that failed during processing  
**Why**: Product reliability, data quality  
**Target**: <1% error rate  
**Tracking**: Real-time, daily summary

```
Submitted: 87
Failed: 0
Error Rate: 0% ✅

If errors spike:
  → Investigate data quality issue
  → Fix bug in processing
  → Notify affected advisors
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Product is reliable
- 🔴 Data quality or code bugs

---

### MONETIZATION METRICS

#### Result Unlock Purchases

**What**: Customers who purchase detailed assessment results/analysis  
**Why**: Product value perception, monetization validation  
**Target**: >20% of assessments → purchase (by month 4)  
**Tracking**: Weekly count, % of completed assessments

```
Completed: 100 assessments
Unlocked Results: 15 purchases
Purchase Rate: 15% (not yet at target)
Revenue: 15 × $15 = $225

Month 4 Target: 25+ purchases
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Customers see value in additional analysis
- 🔴 Free report alone is sufficient, no upsell demand
- 🔴 Price is too high, no willingness to pay

---

#### Analysis Purchases

**What**: Customers who purchase deeper institutional analysis  
**Why**: Premium tier monetization, enterprise pathway  
**Target**: >5% of assessments (by month 6)  
**Tracking**: Monthly count, revenue

```
Completed: 100 assessments
Analysis Purchases: 3
Purchase Rate: 3%
Revenue: 3 × $50 = $150

Month 6 Target: 5+ purchases
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Institutional/advisory market segment exists
- 🔴 No demand for premium analysis
- 🔴 Premium pricing misaligned with value

---

#### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

**What**: Average revenue per advisor (initial + repeat + upsell)  
**Why**: Business model sustainability  
**Target**: $500+ per advisor (by month 6)  
**Tracking**: Per-advisor calculation, monthly average

```
Advisor A: 12 assessments × $15 results = $180 CLV
Advisor B: 18 assessments × $15 results + 2 × $50 analysis = $370 CLV
Advisor C: 5 assessments × 0 purchases = $0 CLV

Average CLV: ($180 + $370 + $0) / 3 = $183
(Below target, need higher attachment rate)
```

**What It Means**:
- 🟢 Advisors generating meaningful revenue
- 🔴 Revenue per advisor too low, business model broken
- 🟡 Revenue growing but not yet sustainable

## MARKET EXPANSION GATES

### Gate 1: Advisor-Direct Traction

**Trigger**: Measurable advisor engagement + revenue

**Requirements**:
- ✅ 20+ activated advisors
- ✅ 5+ assessments/advisor/month
- ✅ >15% purchase rate on results
- ✅ >$2000/month revenue
- ✅ >50% advisor retention

**Decision**: Continue building, scale advisor marketing

**Timeline**: Month 4-6 evaluation

---

### Gate 2: Enterprise Signal

**Trigger**: Market beginning to ask for enterprise features

**Requirements**:
- ✅ 1+ enterprise inquiry
- ✅ 1+ enterprise pilot requested
- ✅ Customer research: enterprises want multi-user / SAML / API

**Decision**: Begin enterprise feature roadmap (but don't build yet)

**Timeline**: Month 6-9 evaluation

---

### Gate 3: Enterprise Contract

**Trigger**: Signed enterprise agreement

**Requirements**:
- ✅ 1+ enterprise customer signed
- ✅ $50K+/year contract value
- ✅ 6+ month commitment (proves not one-off)

**Decision**: Fully build enterprise tier, expand architecture

**Timeline**: Month 12+ (post-MVP market validation)

## Evidence-Based Decision Making

### If Advisor Metrics Are Strong

✅ 20+ advisors, 5+ assessments/month each, >50% retention

**Decision**: Scale advisor marketing, add customer success  
**Don't do**: Build enterprise features yet  
**Rationale**: Market has validated advisor-direct model

---

### If Enterprise Signals Appear Early

✅ 1+ enterprise inquiry in month 2

**Decision**: Interview enterprise prospects, understand requirements  
**Don't do**: Build enterprise features immediately (MVP still incomplete)  
**Rationale**: Signals exist, but validation is missing

---

### If Enterprise Contracts Signed

✅ 1+ enterprise customer $50K+/year

**Decision**: Expand architecture to support multi-user / SAML / API  
**Don't do**: Abandon advisor-direct (it's working)  
**Rationale**: Dual model is viable, scale both

---

### If No Market Traction

❌ <5 advisors by month 3, <1 assessment/advisor/month, 0% retention

**Decision**: Pivot or close  
**Analysis**: Product-market fit is missing  
**Rationale**: Time + capital are finite

## Monthly Analysis Process

**First Friday of each month**:

1. **Compile metrics** (30 min)
   - Aggregate weekly data
   - Calculate rates + trends
   - Compare to targets

2. **Analyze** (30 min)
   - What's working?
   - What's not?
   - Why?

3. **Decide** (30 min)
   - Continue current path?
   - Adjust?
   - Pivot?

4. **Plan** (30 min)
   - What to focus on next month?
   - What gates are we tracking?

5. **Communicate** (30 min)
   - Update team
   - Update advisors (if relevant)
   - Update leadership
