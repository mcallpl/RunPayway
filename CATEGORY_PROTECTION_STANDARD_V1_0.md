## PURPOSE

RunPayway's competitive moat is **category discipline**.

RunPayway is a standardized measurement system.

RunPayway is not:
- Financial advice
- Risk assessment
- Predictive analysis
- Planning guidance
- Coaching
- Consulting
- Behavioral guidance
- Affordability evaluation

The line between "measurement" and "advisory" is precise and defendable.

This standard defines that line and provides rules for every RunPayway output to remain on the measurement side.

**Without category discipline, RunPayway becomes indistinguishable from advisors, AI tools, or risk platforms.**

**With category discipline, RunPayway owns a unique measurement category.**

---

## CATEGORY DEFINITION

**RunPayway is a standardized measurement system for classifying how heavily a financial decision depends on supporting conditions remaining intact.**

RunPayway does not evaluate, advise, predict, or recommend.

RunPayway measures.

---

## SECTION 1: WHAT RUNPAYWAY MEASURES

### Dependence

How much a financial decision relies on specific conditions continuing to be true.

**Measurement approach:** Structural analysis of supporting conditions vs. decision requirements.

**Customer benefit:** Understanding what their decision is built on.

---

### Reliance

What the decision depends on for success.

**Measurement approach:** Identification of primary and secondary supporting factors.

**Customer benefit:** Clarity on what must remain stable.

---

### Support Conditions

The specific circumstances, relationships, income streams, or external factors that the decision requires to function.

**Measurement approach:** Systematic cataloging of requirements.

**Customer benefit:** Understanding what can and cannot change.

---

### Relative Position

Where a decision sits on the dependence spectrum compared to similar decisions.

**Measurement approach:** Comparison to typical situations.

**Customer benefit:** Understanding if their situation is typical, above-typical, or below-typical.

---

### Typical Range

The distribution of dependence classifications in comparable situations.

**Measurement approach:** Aggregation of similar situations to establish range.

**Customer benefit:** Context for their specific classification.

---

### Commitment Pressure™ Classification

The measured level of dependence assigned to a decision.

**Scale:** CPL, CPM, CPE, CPH, CPC
(Low, Moderate, Elevated, High, Critical)

**Measurement approach:** Gap analysis between demand profile and support structure.

**Customer benefit:** A standardized, comparable classification.

## SECTION 2: WHAT RUNPAYWAY DOES NOT MEASURE

### Risk
❌ RunPayway does not measure probability of loss or failure.
**Why excluded:** Risk assessment requires predictive modeling; RunPayway measures structure, not futures.

### Resilience
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate capacity to withstand disruption.
**Why excluded:** Resilience is a judgment; RunPayway measures, not judges.

### Fragility
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate vulnerability to change.
**Why excluded:** Fragility is a judgment; RunPayway measures, not judges.

### Probability
❌ RunPayway does not calculate likelihood of outcomes.
**Why excluded:** Probability requires prediction; RunPayway measures current structure.

### Success
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate likelihood of success.
**Why excluded:** Success evaluation requires prediction; RunPayway measures structure.

### Failure
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate likelihood of failure.
**Why excluded:** Failure prediction requires forward modeling; RunPayway measures current state.

### Safety
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate whether a decision is safe.
**Why excluded:** Safety is a judgment; RunPayway measures structure.

### Affordability
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate whether someone can afford a decision.
**Why excluded:** Affordability is a financial ratio; dependence is structural.

### Creditworthiness
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate credit quality.
**Why excluded:** Creditworthiness is a different measurement category.

### Quality
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate whether a decision is good or bad.
**Why excluded:** Quality is a judgment; RunPayway measures structure.

### Suitability
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate whether a decision is suitable.
**Why excluded:** Suitability is advisory; RunPayway measures.

### Wisdom
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate whether a decision is wise.
**Why excluded:** Wisdom is subjective judgment; RunPayway measures objective structure.

### Financial Strength
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate overall financial health.
**Why excluded:** Financial strength is a comprehensive evaluation; RunPayway measures decision-specific dependence.

### Approval Likelihood
❌ RunPayway does not predict lending approval.
**Why excluded:** Approval prediction is advisory; RunPayway measures structure.

### Investment Value
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate investment return.
**Why excluded:** Investment value is outside the measurement scope.

### Decision Quality
❌ RunPayway does not evaluate whether a decision is good.
**Why excluded:** Decision quality is a judgment; RunPayway measures structure.

## SECTION 3: ALLOWED QUESTIONS

RunPayway is designed to answer these questions only:

1. **"What does this decision depend on?"**
   - RunPayway response: Lists the supporting conditions and primary drivers.

2. **"How heavily does this decision depend on supporting conditions remaining intact?"**
   - RunPayway response: Commitment Pressure classification (CPL-CPC).

3. **"Where does this decision sit compared with similar situations?"**
   - RunPayway response: Position classification (Lower Than Typical, Typical, Higher Than Typical, Meaningfully Higher Than Typical).

4. **"What is typical for comparable situations?"**
   - RunPayway response: Typical Range and Compared With context.

5. **"What are the primary measured drivers of this classification?"**
   - RunPayway response: Primary Drivers section.

6. **"What does this classification mean?"**
   - RunPayway response: Plain-language explanation of the dependence level.

---

## SECTION 4: PROHIBITED QUESTIONS

RunPayway must refuse to answer these questions:

1. **"What should I do?"** ❌
   - This is advice. RunPayway provides measurement, not guidance.

2. **"Should I proceed?"** ❌
   - This is a recommendation. RunPayway measures, not recommends.

3. **"Should I not proceed?"** ❌
   - This is a recommendation. RunPayway measures, not recommends.

4. **"Is this safe?"** ❌
   - This is a judgment. RunPayway measures, not judges.

5. **"Is this risky?"** ❌
   - This is a risk assessment. RunPayway measures dependence, not risk.

6. **"Is this affordable?"** ❌
   - This is an affordability evaluation. RunPayway measures dependence.

7. **"Will this work?"** ❌
   - This is a prediction. RunPayway measures current structure.

8. **"What could go wrong?"** ❌
   - This is risk identification. RunPayway measures structure.

9. **"What should I monitor?"** ❌
   - This is advisory guidance. RunPayway measures, not guides.

10. **"How do I reduce the risk?"** ❌
    - This is consulting. RunPayway measures, not advises.

11. **"How do I improve my classification?"** ❌
    - This is planning guidance. RunPayway measures, not plans.

12. **"How do I make this stronger?"** ❌
    - This is improvement guidance. RunPayway measures, not improves.

13. **"What is my probability of success?"** ❌
    - This is prediction. RunPayway measures, not predicts.

Do not use these terms. Replace them using Section 7 rules.

### Risk Language
❌ risk, risky, riskier, riskiest
❌ risk score, risk level, risk assessment
❌ risk mitigation, reduce risk, lower risk
❌ exposure, vulnerability, threat
❌ warning, red flag, alert

### Judgment Language
❌ safe, unsafe, safer, safer
❌ strong, weak, stronger, weaker
❌ good, bad, better, worse
❌ resilient, fragile, flexible, rigid
❌ robust, brittle, sturdy, unstable

❌ should, should not, must, must not (prescriptive)
❌ should monitor, should watch, should track
❌ should improve, should strengthen, should mitigate
❌ should diversify, should consolidate
❌ should consider, consider, recommend, advisable

### Predictive Language
❌ will, likely, probably, may, might
❌ forecast, predict, prediction
❌ outcome, result, consequence (in predictive context)
❌ sustainable, unsustainable, viable, unviable
❌ likely to succeed, likely to fail
❌ probable, improbable, uncertain

### Planning Language
❌ monitor, watch, track, observe (in guidance context)
❌ improve, enhance, strengthen, optimize
❌ mitigate, reduce, lower, increase
❌ plan for, prepare for, plan to
❌ strategy, strategic, tactical

### Affordability Language
❌ afford, affordable, unaffordable
❌ can pay, cannot pay
❌ sufficient, insufficient (in financial context)
❌ adequate, inadequate (in financial context)

### Failure Language
❌ fail, failure, succeed, success (as predictions)
❌ work, won't work (as predictions)
❌ break, breaks, broken (in predictive context)

## SECTION 7: REPLACEMENT RULES

When you encounter prohibited language, apply these replacements:

### Risk Language Replacements

**Bad:** "This decision is risky."
**Good:** "This decision carries High Commitment Pressure."

**Bad:** "There is significant risk in this decision."
**Good:** "This decision depends heavily on several supporting conditions."

**Bad:** "Risk exposure is high."
**Good:** "The decision depends on multiple important conditions continuing."

**Bad:** "Risk mitigation is important."
**Good:** "Understanding what the decision depends on is important."

---

### Judgment Language Replacements

**Bad:** "This decision is strong."
**Good:** "This decision has limited dependence on external conditions."

**Bad:** "This decision is weak."
**Good:** "This decision depends on several important conditions."

**Bad:** "This structure is fragile."
**Good:** "This decision has limited support breadth."

**Bad:** "This structure is resilient."
**Good:** "This decision has flexible support sources."

**Bad:** "This is a good decision."
**Good:** "This decision carries Moderate Commitment Pressure."

---

### Advisory Language Replacements

**Bad:** "You should monitor cash flow."
**Good:** "The decision depends on continued cash flow."

**Bad:** "You should diversify income."
**Good:** "Support is concentrated in a single income source."

**Bad:** "Consider reducing risk."
**Good:** "The decision depends on several important conditions."

**Bad:** "This is not suitable for you."
**Good:** "This decision carries Critical Commitment Pressure."

---

### Predictive Language Replacements

**Bad:** "This decision will likely succeed."
**Good:** "This decision carries Low Commitment Pressure."

**Bad:** "This plan may not be sustainable."
**Good:** "This decision depends on several important supporting conditions."

**Bad:** "This may fail if income drops."
**Good:** "The decision relies heavily on continued income."

---

### Planning Language Replacements

**Bad:** "You should improve this before proceeding."
**Good:** "Understand what this decision depends on before committing."

**Bad:** "Plan to monitor this situation."
**Good:** "These factors are what the decision depends on."

**Bad:** "Track these metrics going forward."
**Good:** "These are the primary drivers of the classification."

## SECTION 8: REPORT OUTPUT RULES

Apply category protection to each section of the Decision Check™ report:

### 1. MEASUREMENT Section

**Allowed:**
- Classification name only (Low, Moderate, Elevated, High, Critical)
- No explanation
- No justification

**Prohibited:**
- Any advisory language
- Any risk language
- Any judgment language
- Any monitoring language

**Example (✅ Correct):**
```
Measurement
Elevated Commitment Pressure
```

**Example (❌ Wrong):**
```
Measurement
Elevated Commitment Pressure (you should monitor this)
```

---

### 2. POSITION Section

**Allowed:**
- Locked placement label only
- No explanation
- No context

**Prohibited:**
- Any explanation
- Any judgment
- Any advisory language

**Example (✅ Correct):**
```
Position
Higher Than Typical
```

---

### 3. COMPARED WITH Section

**Allowed:**
- Decision Type + Archetype Translation
- Fixed structure: "[Decision Type] supported by [Archetype]."
- No explanation
- No judgment

**Prohibited:**
- Risk language
- Advisory language
- Judgment language

**Example (✅ Correct):**
```
Compared With
Home purchases supported by employment income.
```

---

### 4. INTERPRETATION Section

**Allowed:**
- Structural explanation only
- What the decision depends on
- Plain language description
- No recommendations
- No risk language
- No judgment

**Prohibited:**
- "Should monitor"
- "Risk assessment"
- Safe/unsafe
- Good/bad judgment
- "Will work/fail" prediction

**Common Drift Risk:**
Adding "what you should monitor" or "what could go wrong"

**Example (✅ Correct):**
```
Interpretation
This home purchase relies meaningfully on the continued strength of 
the structure supporting it. Commission income must persist at 
expected levels throughout the mortgage term.
```

**Example (❌ Wrong - Monitoring Drift):**
```
Interpretation
This home purchase relies on continued income. You should monitor 
your commission levels quarterly and watch for any decline in client 
projects. This could threaten the mortgage if it changes.
```

---

### 5. PRIMARY DRIVERS Section

**Allowed:**
- Structural facts
- What the decision depends on
- Why the classification is correct
- Plain language

**Prohibited:**
- Risk language
- Advisory language
- Monitoring language
- Recommendations

**Common Drift Risk:**
"You should..." language

**Example (✅ Correct):**
```
Primary Drivers

• Support depends on variable income continuation
• Long-term horizon extends dependence on sustained income
• Loss of income would stress mortgage capacity
• Employment alone insufficient to cover full obligation
```

**Example (❌ Wrong - Advisory Drift):**
```
Primary Drivers

• You should maintain your commission income
• You should avoid major disruptions
• You should consider diversifying income
• You should monitor client projects closely
```

---

### 6. IMPLICATIONS Section

**Allowed:**
- Structural consequence statement
- What must continue to go right
- Plain language

**Prohibited:**
- Recommendations
- Action plans
- Monitoring guidance
- Risk language
- Safe/unsafe

**Example (✅ Correct):**
```
Implications
Several important things must continue to go right for this home 
purchase to remain supported.
```

**Example (❌ Wrong - Advisory Drift):**
```
Implications
You should monitor your income carefully and consider contingency 
planning. If income drops, you may face difficulties with the 
mortgage.
```

---

### 7. TYPICAL RANGE Section

**Allowed:**
- Fixed structure only: "Most [situations] fall between X and Y Commitment Pressure."
- No explanation
- No judgment

**Prohibited:**
- Explanation of the range
- Risk language
- Advisory language

**Example (✅ Correct):**
```
Typical Range
Most home purchases supported by employment income fall between 
Moderate and Elevated Commitment Pressure.
```

---

### 8. TECHNICAL CLASSIFICATION Section

**Allowed:**
- Code display only
- No explanation

**Prohibited:**
- Any explanation
- Any context

**Example (✅ Correct):**
```
Technical Classification

CPE
```

## SECTION 10: ADVISOR / PROFESSIONAL MATERIAL RULES

Advisor materials must maintain category discipline even when communicating with professionals.

### Key Rule

**Even to professionals, RunPayway does not recommend.**

RunPayway may:
- Explain measurement results
- Provide context for client conversations
- Document structural analysis
- Support professional judgment

RunPayway must not:
- Recommend actions
- Suggest clients change decisions
- Advise on improvements
- Provide risk guidance

### Advisor Copy Standards

**Allowed:**
- "Help your client understand what their decision depends on"
- "Provide structural context for your planning"
- "Document the measured dependence"
- "Use this as a starting point for your discussion"

**Prohibited:**
- "Tell your client what to do"
- "Advise your client to reduce risk"
- "Recommend structural changes"
- "Provide risk guidance"

   - If no: Use approved vocabulary replacements.
   - If yes: Continue.
