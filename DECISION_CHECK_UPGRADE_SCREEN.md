People don't value features. They value outcomes.

---

## Why "Interpretation" Not "Analysis"

NOT: "Advanced analysis of your income"
YES: "Full structural interpretation"

"Analysis" feels technical. "Interpretation" feels like understanding.

---

## Why "Framework" Not "Tools"

NOT: "Use our tools to make better decisions"
YES: "Learn how to think about commitments with income like yours"

Framework teaches thinking. Tools are widgets.

## Why "Application to Your Decision" Not "Home Purchase Calculator"

NOT: "Calculate how much house you can afford"
YES: "How your specific structure affects your home purchase decision"

One is a tool. One is understanding applied to your life.

Every sentence emphasizes:
- Understanding vs. advice
- Structure vs. products
- Interpretation vs. calculation
- Framework vs. tools
- Clarity vs. qualification

This positions RunPayway as the income structure standard—not a fintech tool, not a mortgage helper, not an advisor.
