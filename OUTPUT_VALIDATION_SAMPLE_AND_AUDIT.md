# Decision Check™ Output Validation Audit™ v1.0

**Status**: PRODUCT VALIDATION SAMPLE  
**Date**: June 19, 2026  
**Purpose**: Determine whether locked standards generate meaningful, valuable output  

---

## METHODOLOGY

**Sample Size**: 50 complete Decision Check™ reports

**Coverage**:
- 10 Decision Types (5 reports each)
- 5 Commitment Pressure levels (CPL, CPM, CPE, CPH, CPC)
- Mixed archetype representations
- Realistic scenarios

**Validation Criteria**:
1. Consumer Value — Would a consumer perceive meaningful insight?
2. Professional Value — Would professionals find it useful?
3. Differentiation — Does it feel distinct from existing tools?
4. Category Protection — Remains measurement-only?
5. Institutional Credibility — Feels like professional analysis?
