# PART 1: PURPOSE (ONE SENTENCE)

## Decision Check™ Purpose

**Decision Check™ explains what characteristics of your income structure are relevant to a specific financial decision, so you understand the structural factors that matter most to your situation.**

---

# PART 2: WHAT THE REPORT MUST INCLUDE TO FEEL VALUABLE

## Required Elements for Premium Perception

### 1. Income Structure Summary (Baseline)
- What you earn
- Where it comes from
- How stable it is
- What makes it complex or unique

**Why valuable**: Customer sees the data clearly (no guessing)

### 2. Decision-Specific Context
- Why THIS income structure matters for THIS decision
- What factors lenders/advisors/institutions focus on
- How your specific structure relates to the decision

**Why valuable**: Customer gets decision-relevant insight (not generic)

### 3. Structural Characteristics That Matter
- Which RP-2.0 metrics are most relevant
- What they mean in plain language
- Why each one matters for this decision

**Why valuable**: Customer learns technical understanding (feels premium)

### 4. What Lenders/Institutions Look For
- For Home Purchase: What mortgage underwriters prioritize
- For Career Change: What considerations matter
- For Business Launch: What revenue understanding is critical
- For Education: What income stability means
- For Investment Property: What asset income shows

**Why valuable**: Customer gets insider perspective (feels credible)

### 5. Decision-Relevant Preparation Guidance
- What documentation you may need
- What conversations to have
- What to understand before proceeding
- NOT: "You should do this" BUT "Institutions typically look for this"

**Why valuable**: Customer is prepared (feels actionable without advice)

### 6. Clear Structural Takeaway
- In one paragraph: What your income structure reveals about this decision
- Measurement-only, no prediction

**Why valuable**: Clarity (customer understands the structural picture)

### 7. No Recommendations, No Predictions
- Explicit statement that this is measurement, not advice
- Clarifies what RunPayway does NOT do

**Why valuable**: Credibility (customer knows this is measurement-grade)

# PART 4: FOR EACH DECISION TYPE, DEFINE THE VALUE

## 1. HOME PURCHASE

### Income Characteristics That Matter Most

**Primary**: Stability Band (lenders use this)  
**Secondary**: Labor Dependence % (employment vs. passive)  
**Tertiary**: Largest Source % (concentration)  
**Quaternary**: Forward Secured % (guaranteed vs. variable)  

### What the Report Should Explain

"For a mortgage lender, income stability and predictability are paramount. This report shows how your income structure aligns with what lenders typically look for: steady employment income, documented history, and clear debt-service capacity."

**Focus on**:
- Employment income is primary qualification factor
- Income variability affects debt-to-income ratio
- Down payment sources (asset income matters here)
- Employment stability history matters

### What Language to AVOID

❌ "You qualify" or "You don't qualify"  
❌ "You can afford this"  
❌ "Your approval chances are"  
❌ "You should/shouldn't buy"  
❌ "Your creditworthiness"  

### Approved Phrasing

✅ "Lenders typically focus on..."  
✅ "Your income structure shows..."  
✅ "This characteristic may require..."  
✅ "Institutional standards typically prioritize..."  
✅ "Documentation you should prepare..."  

### What Customer Should Understand After Reading

"I understand why my income structure matters for this purchase. I know what a mortgage lender will focus on. I understand what documentation I need and what conversations to have with the lender. I'm prepared to discuss my income structure with confidence."

## Software Sales + Home Purchase

### Why This Example Matters

Software sales engineers often have:
- W-2 base salary + commission/bonus (mixed but employment-based)

                  • $116,000 guaranteed base (salary +

Why it matters:   Lenders typically include base compensation

                  compensation. Your 80% guaranteed portion

Your situation:   Your $116,000 base provides your mortgage 
                  foundation. Your $29,000 variable component

                  depending on documentation history.

What it means:    Commission/bonus income requires documented 
                  history to be included in qualification. 
                  Lenders want to verify it's recurring and

• Base Salary Stability: Lenders prioritize your W-2 base salary

  matters. Established tech companies are favorable.

• Commission Consistency: Lenders will ask: Is your commission 
  consistent year-over-year? Has it grown, stayed flat, or 
  declined? Your B-band stability suggests consistency is in 
  your favor.

• Income Documentation: For mortgage qualification, you'll provide:
  - Last 2 years tax returns (showing commission + base split)
  - Last 2 months pay stubs (showing base + current period 
    commission)
  - Offer letter or employment contract (describing commission 
    structure)
  - YTD income statement from employer
  
  This documentation typically strengthens commission income 
  consideration.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  ✓ Is your base salary $116,000 stable and continuing?
  ✓ Is your commission structure clearly documented?
  ✓ Have you been in this role long enough to show commission

  ✓ Your stability band (B) reflects your actual income history?
