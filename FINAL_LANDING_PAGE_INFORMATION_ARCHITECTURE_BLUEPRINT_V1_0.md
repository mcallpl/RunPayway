## Issue #1: "Monitor" Language Throughout

### Violation Type
**Planning/Advisory Drift** (Category Protection Standard Section 6 & Section 4, Question 9)

### Location
Landing Page Translation Implementation v1.0, Throughout (acceptable but requires consistency)

### Current Language
"What must continue to go right" and "What must continue" are used interchangeably

### Compliance Status

Standardize to "What the decision depends on" throughout landing page for maximum clarity.
