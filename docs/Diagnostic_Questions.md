# RunPayway™ Diagnostic Questions

## Overview

RunPayway™ uses a standardized 6-question diagnostic to assess income structure across three audience levels:
- **Consumer Questions** — Direct, plain-language framing for self-assessment
- **Advisor Questions** — Professional framing for financial advisors assessing clients
- **Free Assessment Questions** — Simplified language for first-time visitors

All three versions map to the same A/B/C/D/E answer scale and feed the same diagnostic engine.

---

## Consumer Diagnostic Questions

These questions are presented directly to business owners and professionals assessing their own income structure.

### Question 1: Recurring Revenue Base
**What percentage of your income renews automatically through an existing agreement or subscription?**

*Note: Recurring income renews without requiring new client acquisition.*

- **A** — 0–10%
- **B** — 11–30%
- **C** — 31–60%
- **D** — 61–85%
- **E** — 86–100%

---

### Question 2: Income Source Diversification
**Over the previous 12 months, how spread out was your income across different sources?**

*Note: Think about how much of your total income depended on any single source, such as a top client.*

- **A** — Almost all from one source (90–100%)
- **B** — Mostly from one source (70–89%)
- **C** — About half from one source (50–69%)
- **D** — Spread across a few sources (30–49% from largest)
- **E** — Well diversified (under 30% from any single source)

---

### Question 3: Income Source Count
**Over the previous 12 months, how many separate income sources each contributed at least 10% of your total income?**

*Note: Only count sources that contributed 10% or more.*

- **A** — 1
- **B** — 2
- **C** — 3–4
- **D** — 5–7
- **E** — 8 or more

---

### Question 4: Forward Revenue Visibility
**How many months of future income are currently secured — such as forward commitments?**

*Note: Only include income that is already contractually committed.*

- **A** — Less than 1 month
- **B** — 1–2 months
- **C** — 3–5 months
- **D** — 6–11 months
- **E** — 12 or more months

---

### Question 5: Earnings Consistency
**Over the previous 12 months, how consistent was your monthly income?**

*Note: Compare your highest and lowest earning months relative to your average.*

- **A** — Very inconsistent (fluctuated more than 75%)
- **B** — Inconsistent (fluctuated 50–75%)
- **C** — Somewhat consistent (fluctuated 25–49%)
- **D** — Mostly consistent (fluctuated 10–24%)
- **E** — Very consistent (fluctuated less than 10%)

---

### Question 6: Income Without Active Work
**If you stopped active work for 90 consecutive days, what percentage of your income would continue automatically?**

*Note: Income that continues without active work or new client acquisition.*

- **A** — 0%
- **B** — 1–25%
- **C** — 26–50%
- **D** — 51–75%
- **E** — 76–100%

## Advisor Diagnostic Questions

These questions are professionally framed for financial advisors and wealth professionals assessing their clients' income structure.

### Question 1: Recurring Revenue Base
**What portion of your client's income renews automatically — through existing agreements, renewals, or subscriptions — without requiring new client acquisition?**

*Note: Do not include income that requires re-selling or re-earning each period.*

- **A** — None — income is entirely transactional (0–10%)
- **B** — A small recurring component (11–30%)
- **C** — A moderate recurring base (31–60%)
- **D** — A strong recurring foundation (61–85%)
- **E** — Primarily recurring — over 85% auto-renews

---

### Question 2: Income Concentration
**How concentrated is your client's income around a single source — top client, employer, transaction type, or platform?**

*Note: Consider the share from the single largest contributor over the past 12 months.*

- **A** — Extremely concentrated — 90–100% from one source
- **B** — Highly concentrated — 70–89% from one source
- **C** — Moderately concentrated — 50–69% from one source
- **D** — Moderately distributed — 30–49% from the largest source
- **E** — Well distributed — under 30% from any single source

---

### Question 3: Source Diversification
**How many distinct income sources — each representing at least 10% of total income — does your client have?**

*Note: Count only sources that independently contribute 10% or more.*

- **A** — One
- **B** — Two
- **C** — Three or four
- **D** — Five to seven
- **E** — Eight or more

---

### Question 4: Forward Revenue Visibility
**How much of your client's upcoming income is already secured through signed contracts, binding renewals, or committed forward agreements?**

*Note: Include only legally committed or contractually obligated income — not projections or expected renewals.*

- **A** — Less than one month of committed forward income
- **B** — One to two months secured
- **C** — Three to five months secured
- **D** — Six to eleven months secured
- **E** — Twelve or more months secured

---

### Question 5: Earnings Consistency
**Over the past 12 months, how variable has your client's monthly income been?**

*Note: Assess the range between their highest and lowest earning months relative to their average.*

- **A** — Extreme variability — fluctuations exceeding 75%
- **B** — High variability — fluctuations of 50–75%
- **C** — Moderate variability — fluctuations of 25–49%
- **D** — Low variability — fluctuations of 10–24%
- **E** — Consistent — fluctuations under 10%

---

### Question 6: Labor Independence
**If your client stopped actively working for 90 consecutive days, what percentage of their income would continue to arrive without direct intervention?**

*Note: Include only income streams that operate independently of active effort — contracts, renewals, passive distributions.*

- **A** — None — income is entirely dependent on active labor (0%)
- **B** — A small portion continues independently (1–25%)
- **C** — About half continues without active effort (26–50%)
- **D** — More than half continues independently (51–75%)
- **E** — Nearly all income continues without intervention (76–100%)

## Free Assessment Questions

These simplified questions are designed for first-time visitors who don't yet understand income structure terminology. They use plain language and avoid percentages.

### Question 1: How reliable is your income each month?
**How much of your income comes back automatically — without you re-earning it?**

*Hint: Think about retainers, subscriptions, or contracts that renew on their own.*

- **A** — Almost none — I start from zero every month
- **B** — A small portion — maybe 10 to 30%
- **C** — About a third to half of it
- **D** — More than half — mostly ongoing agreements
- **E** — Almost all of it continues without me re-earning it

---

### Question 2: How spread out is your income?
**How much of your income comes from your single biggest source?**

*Hint: This might be your largest client, employer, platform, or income type.*

- **A** — Nearly all of it — one source makes up almost everything
- **B** — Most of it — more than two thirds
- **C** — About half
- **D** — Less than half — spread across a few sources
- **E** — A small portion — income is well distributed

---

### Question 3: How many sources contribute to your income?
**How many separate sources each bring in a meaningful share of your total income?**

*Hint: Only count sources that represent at least 10% of your total.*

- **A** — One
- **B** — Two
- **C** — Three or four
- **D** — Five to seven
- **E** — Eight or more

---

### Question 4: How far ahead is your income locked in?
**How much of your upcoming income is already secured — from signed deals, contracts, or renewals?**

*Hint: Only count income that is already committed, not expected or hoped for.*

- **A** — Less than a month — I don't know what's coming
- **B** — A month or two ahead
- **C** — Three to five months ahead
- **D** — Six months to a year ahead
- **E** — A year or more ahead

---

### Question 5: How consistent is your income month to month?
**Looking at your best and worst months in the past year — how much did your income swing?**

*Hint: Think about the gap between your highest and lowest earning months.*

- **A** — Dramatically — some months were 3 to 5 times others
- **B** — A lot — swings of 50 to 75%
- **C** — Noticeably — swings of 25 to 50%
- **D** — Somewhat — minor swings of 10 to 25%
- **E** — Very little — roughly the same each month

---

### Question 6: What happens if you stop working?
**If you couldn't work for 90 days, what portion of your income would keep coming in on its own?**

*Hint: Think about any income that continues regardless of your active involvement.*

- **A** — None — income stops completely without me
- **B** — A small amount — maybe 10 to 25% continues
- **C** — About half continues on its own
- **D** — More than half — most income runs without me
- **E** — Almost all of it continues without me
