# RunPayway™ Content Governance

**Institutional Tone, Canonical Terminology, Semantic Authority**

Content governance is not about lists of banned words. It's about tone philosophy, semantic clarity, and institutional voice that separates RunPayway™ from generic fintech marketing.

---

## TONE PHILOSOPHY

RunPayway™ copy should feel:

### ✓ Composed
- Calm, not caffeinated
- Methodical, not frantic
- Confident without desperation
- Measured pace, clear progression

**Example:**
```
✓ "RunPayway™ evaluates the structure behind your income."
✗ "Unlock the hidden potential in your financial situation!"
```

### ✓ Analytical
- Facts over feelings
- Structure over narrative
- Methodology over promises
- Clarity over persuasion

**Example:**
```
✓ "Our fixed methodology evaluates income continuity through structural analysis."
✗ "See how our cutting-edge AI reveals your true financial power."
```

### ✓ Restrained
- Minimal hype
- No exaggeration
- Honest about scope
- Clear about what we do and don't do

**Example:**
```
✓ "RunPayway™ assesses income structure, not future earnings."
✗ "Transform your financial future with our revolutionary system!"
```

### ✓ Institutionally Confident
- Authority without arrogance
- Expertise without lectures
- Leadership without preaching
- Standards without gatekeeping

**Example:**
```
✓ "We use a fixed, version-stamped methodology. Same inputs produce the same result."
✗ "Our proprietary technology gives you insights nobody else can."
```

### ✓ Structurally Precise
- Exact language about what we evaluate
- No vague generalities
- Technical clarity without jargon
- Specificity about scope

**Example:**
```
✓ "We evaluate income structure: continuity, concentration, dependency patterns, and forward visibility."
✗ "We analyze your overall financial health and stability."
```

### ✓ Human Enough to Understand Quickly
- Plain English
- Short sentences
- Active voice
- Clarity prioritized over eloquence

**Example:**
```
✓ "Assess your income structure before making major financial decisions."
✗ "Undertake a comprehensive examination of your income's foundational composition prior to committing to substantial pecuniary obligations."
```

## BANNED LANGUAGE

### Strictly Prohibited (Never Use)

**Motivational Finance Language:**
- ❌ "Unlock your financial potential"
- ❌ "Take control of your finances"
- ❌ "Transform your financial future"
- ❌ "Financial freedom"
- ❌ "Empower yourself financially"
- ❌ "Level up your financial life"

**Hype & Exaggeration:**
- ❌ "Revolutionary"
- ❌ "Game-changing"
- ❌ "Next-generation"
- ❌ "Cutting-edge"
- ❌ "Breakthrough"
- ❌ "Disruptive"
- ❌ "The future of [anything]"

**AI-Centered Language:**
- ❌ "AI-powered"
- ❌ "Artificial intelligence"
- ❌ "Machine learning"
- ❌ "Powered by advanced algorithms"
- ❌ "Neural networks" (in marketing copy)
- ❌ "Big data insights" (this is marketing, not description)

**Vague Consultant Language:**
- ❌ "Synergy"
- ❌ "Holistic approach"
- ❌ "Leverage" (as verb, in marketing)
- ❌ "Optimization"
- ❌ "Streamline"
- ❌ "Maximize"
- ❌ "Enhanced"

**Startup Conversion Energy:**
- ❌ "Limited time offer"
- ❌ "Act now"
- ❌ "Don't miss out"
- ❌ "Get started today"
- ❌ "Only [X] spots left"
- ❌ "Join thousands of satisfied users"

**Fake Credibility:**
- ❌ "Trusted by"
- ❌ "Join [number] users who..."
- ❌ "Proven methodology" (cite it, don't claim it)
- ❌ "Award-winning"
- ❌ "Industry leader"
- ❌ "Recognized by"

**Unfounded Transformation Claims:**
- ❌ "Change your life"
- ❌ "Achieve financial security"
- ❌ "Guarantee[s]"
- ❌ "Ensure success"
- ❌ "Never worry about money again"
- ❌ "Completely transform"

**Generic Finance Jargon:**
- ❌ "Financial stability" (use "income stability")
- ❌ "Income verification" (use "structural verification")
- ❌ "Risk assessment" (use "structural risk assessment")
- ❌ "Financial health"
- ❌ "Money management"
- ❌ "Financial wellness"

### Discouraged (Use Sparingly, Never in Headlines)

**Subjective Value Language:**
- "Unique" — OK in specific context, avoid general use
- "Powerful" — Only for technical capability, not general messaging
- "Simple" — OK if genuinely simple; avoid if oversimplifying
- "Easy" — OK for user experience, avoid for complex methodology

**Enthusiasm Modifiers:**
- Exclamation marks — Maximum 1-2 per page, only in CTAs
- All caps emphasis — Avoid entirely
- "Wow" or "Amazing" — Never
- Multiple punctuation — Never

**Future Tense Promises:**
- "Will help you" — OK, but specific is better
- "Can assist with" — Accurate, but less confident
- "May allow" — Too hedged for authoritative messaging

## APPROVED VOCABULARY

### Always Use (Canonical Terms)

| Concept | Approved | Avoid | Why |
|---------|----------|-------|-----|
| Core methodology | Structural verification | Income verification, financial analysis | Specificity: we verify structure, not identity or prediction |
| Income assessment | Income stability | Financial stability, financial health | Precise: we assess income specifically |
| How we operate | Fixed methodology, version-stamped | Proprietary, advanced, intelligent | Transparency: fixed = reproducible |
| Results | Deterministic results | Insights, analytics, reports | Accuracy: deterministic = same input = same output |
| Assessment scope | Income structure | Income profile, financial situation | Technical precision |
| What we evaluate | Structural factors | Financial factors | Specificity: we evaluate structure |
| Methodology nature | Institutional framework | Cutting-edge platform | Authority: framework = established, thoughtful |
| Assessment depth | Structural analysis | Deep dive, comprehensive analysis | Precision without exaggeration |
| Results quality | Version-stamped, repeatable | Consistent, reliable | Accuracy: version-stamped is specific |

### Examples of Preferred Transformation

**Instead of startup motivational copy:**
```
BAD:
"Unlock your financial potential with our AI-powered insights. Take control of your future."

BETTER:
"Evaluate your income structure before major financial decisions. Understand the risks. Make informed choices."
```

**Instead of generic finance language:**
```
BAD:
"Our platform provides comprehensive financial analysis to help manage your money better."

BETTER:
"RunPayway™ uses structural verification to assess income continuity, concentration, and forward visibility."
```

**Instead of consultant jargon:**
```
BAD:
"Synergistic financial optimization through leverage of advanced analytics."

BETTER:
"Fixed methodology. Version-stamped results. Same inputs always produce the same output."
```

**Instead of overpromising:**
```
BAD:
"Transform your financial life and achieve complete security."

BETTER:
"Make financial decisions with clear understanding of your income structure and risks."
```

**Instead of generic authority signaling:**
```
BAD:
"Join thousands of users who trust our platform."

BETTER:
"RunPayway™ is the institutional framework for structural income verification used by advisors and organizations."
```

## TONE IN CONTEXT

### How It Works Page Tone
- Authority + Methodological clarity
- "Fixed methodology that evaluates..."
- "Version-stamped results..."
- "Deterministic assessment..."

### Use Case Pages Tone
- Practical + Structural clarity
- "How structural verification helps with [decision]..."
- "Assess the structural factors that affect..."
- "Understand the continuity risks in [context]..."

### Learn Pages Tone
- Educational + Precise
- Define terms clearly
- Explain why it matters
- Connect to larger methodology
- No sales language

### CTA Copy Tone
- Action-oriented + Clear
- "Get Structural Assessment"
- "See Results"
- "Schedule Review"
- "Begin Assessment"
- Slightly more assertive than body copy, but not pushy

### Footer/Navigation Tone
- Minimal, functional
- Links are descriptive, not enticing
- No "Discover," "Explore," "Learn" as links alone
- Specificity: "How It Works" not "Learn More"

## VOICE PRINCIPLES

### Perspective
- First person plural when referring to RunPayway™ ("we" and "our")
- Active voice preferred
- Direct address to reader when appropriate

**Examples:**
```
✓ "We use a fixed methodology..."
✓ "RunPayway™ evaluates..."
✓ "You can assess..."
✗ "It is believed that..."
✗ "One might consider..."
```

### Urgency
- Avoid all artificial urgency
- Focus on clarity and value, not scarcity
- Allow decision-making time

**Examples:**
```
✗ "Limited spots available"
✗ "Don't miss out"
✗ "Act now before..."
✓ "Get started when you're ready"
✓ "Schedule a review at your convenience"
```

### Credibility
- Cite methodology, not celebrity
- Show work, don't claim expertise
- Be transparent about scope
- Avoid vague trust language

**Examples:**
```
✗ "Trusted by thousands"
✗ "Industry leader"
✓ "Used by advisors and organizations"
✓ "Our fixed methodology evaluates..."
✓ "We assess income structure, not future earnings"
```

### Simplicity
- Short sentences for accessibility
- Technical accuracy without jargon
- If technical term needed, define it
- Plain English first choice

**Examples:**
```
✗ "Undertake a comprehensive structural assessment of your income composition"
✓ "Assess your income structure"
```

## SENTENCE STRUCTURE

### Length & Rhythm
- Vary sentence length, but lean short
- Break complex ideas into steps
- Paragraph length: 2-4 sentences maximum
- Scannable by readers on phones

**Example:**
```
Good:
"RunPayway™ evaluates income structure through three core factors: continuity, concentration, and forward visibility. Continuity answers: Will this income persist? Concentration answers: How dependent am I on few sources? Forward visibility answers: Can I assess future stability?"

Bad:
"Through our comprehensive proprietary analysis of the multifaceted dimensions of income composition, including but not limited to continuity, concentration patterns, and forward visibility assessments, RunPayway™ provides insights into structural stability."
```

### Active Voice
- Default to active voice
- Use passive only when subject is unknown or irrelevant
- Subject + verb + object clarity

**Examples:**
```
✓ "We evaluate income structure."
✓ "RunPayway™ assesses deterministic results."
✗ "Income structure is evaluated."
✗ "A comprehensive assessment is conducted."
```

### Clarity Over Elegance
- Direct > Ornate
- Specific > General
- Plain > Fancy

**Examples:**
```
✓ "This helps your mortgage application."
✓ "We evaluate three factors."
✓ "Results are repeatable."
✗ "This facilitates favorable outcomes in residential lending scenarios."
✗ "A multifaceted assessment encompasses several dimensions."
✗ "Consistent methodology yields comparable results."
```

## NUMBERS & SPECIFICITY

### Citing Data
- Be specific, not rounded
- Cite methodology, not opinion
- Show your work

**Examples:**
```
✓ "Our fixed methodology evaluates three structural factors: continuity, concentration, and forward visibility."
✓ "Assessment takes under 2 minutes."
✗ "Thousands of users benefit from our platform."
✗ "Get industry-leading insights today."
```

### Timelines
- Specific, not vague
- Realistic, not rushed
- Include methodology, not just speed

**Examples:**
```
✓ "Assessment takes under 2 minutes."
✓ "Results are version-stamped to show methodology consistency."
✗ "Get instant results."
✗ "Immediately unlock insights."
```

## REVIEW CHECKLIST

Before publishing copy, ask:

- [ ] Does this use canonical terminology only?
- [ ] Are there any banned words or phrases?
- [ ] Does the tone feel composed and analytical?
- [ ] Is the language specific or vague?
- [ ] Are there exclamation marks outside CTAs?
- [ ] Does it overpromise or overstate?
- [ ] Is it institutional confidence or arrogance?
- [ ] Could an AI-generated version be indistinguishable from this?
- [ ] Does it sound like RunPayway™ or a generic fintech company?
- [ ] If read aloud, does it feel natural?
