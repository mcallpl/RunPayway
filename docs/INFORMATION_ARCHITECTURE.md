# RunPayway™ Information Architecture

**System-Wide Knowledge, Trust, Verification, and Conversion Flow**

RunPayway™ is not a company website. It is an institutional platform that teaches a language, builds methodological trust, and guides users through structural verification. This document defines how all pages, concepts, and user paths connect as a unified system.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

RunPayway™ operates as four integrated layers:

1. **Trust Layer** — Pages that establish institutional credibility and methodological authority
2. **Learning Layer** — Pages that teach structural income language and concepts
3. **Verification Layer** — Pages that guide users through structural assessment
4. **Application Layer** — Pages that show practical use in industries and decisions

Users enter at different points and follow different paths, but all paths eventually converge on structural understanding and verification opportunity. The architecture ensures that no matter where users enter, they progress through trust-building → learning → verification → application.

---

## CORE ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLES

### 1. Language as Infrastructure

RunPayway™ is creating a new vocabulary. The architecture must reinforce that language constantly:

- **Income Stability** — Not "financial health" or "financial security"
- **Income Structure** — Not "income profile" or "income situation"
- **Structural Verification** — Not "income verification" or "assessment"
- **Deterministic Results** — Not "consistent" or "reliable"
- **Version-Stamped Output** — Not "reliable" or "trustworthy"
- **Stability Classification** — Not "rating" or "score"

Every page reinforces these terms. Glossary pages define them. Use case pages apply them. Methodology pages explain them.

### 2. Trust Progression, Not Conversion Funnel

Traditional funnels are aggressive and transactional. RunPayway™ uses trust progression:

- **Entry** — User arrives with skepticism or curiosity
- **First Trust** — User understands what we do (methodology clarity)
- **Deepening Trust** — User understands why it matters (relevance to their situation)
- **Verification Trust** — User sees structural insight (free result preview)
- **Adoption** — User engages with full verification (if appropriate for their situation)

Not every user converts to paid. That's intentional. Users who learn and don't need verification are still community members who understand the language.

### 3. Concept as Foundation

Every page is built on conceptual scaffolding, not marketing copy. Concepts build from simple to complex:

**Level 1: Foundation** — "Income and income stability are not the same thing"
**Level 2: Structure** — Income has structure: sources, patterns, dependencies
**Level 3: Analysis** — We can analyze structure: concentration, continuity, visibility
**Level 4: Methodology** — We use fixed methodology: deterministic, repeatable, version-stamped
**Level 5: Application** — Apply to your decision: mortgage, career, business, retirement

### 4. Methodology as Support, Not Sales

Methodology pages are not sales tools. They are support infrastructure.

- Use case pages link back to methodology for conceptual support
- Verification pages reference methodology for confidence
- Advisor pages cite methodology for credibility
- Organization pages reference methodology for institutional validation

Methodology is called upon when users need reassurance or deeper understanding.

### 5. Glossary as Semantic Foundation

The glossary is not decorative or secondary. It is the semantic foundation of the entire platform.

- Every glossary term has a canonical definition
- Every glossary term links to use cases where it matters
- Every glossary term connects to industries where it applies
- Glossary pages are authority anchors for semantic linking

When a use case page says "concentration," it links to the glossary definition. That link serves three purposes:
1. User can deepen understanding if needed
2. Search engines understand semantic relationships
3. The system reinforces vocabulary

### 6. No Orphan Pages

Every page must serve a purpose in the user's journey. Every page must have:

- An entry point (where users arrive)
- A supporting role (what understanding does it build?)
- A next page (where does it lead?)
- Contextual links (what supports this understanding?)

Pages that don't serve this architecture are removed or consolidated.

## CONCEPTUAL DEEPENING PATH (Learning Progression)

Users progress from simple concept to complex application. The architecture ensures concepts are introduced simply and deepened progressively.

### Level 1: Foundation Concept

**Core Realization:** "Income and income stability are not the same thing"

**Where introduced:**
- Landing page (initial positioning)
- How It Works (first section)
- Use case pages (immediate relevance)

**Simple explanation:** You can have income without stability. What matters is whether that income will continue.

**Example:** Someone with variable consulting income has income, but uncertain stability.

---

### Level 2: Structure Concept

**Core Realization:** "Income has structure you can analyze"

**Where introduced:**
- How It Works (second section)
- Glossary: Income Structure page
- Industry pages (industry-specific structures)

**Simple explanation:** Income comes from sources, has patterns, has dependencies. You can map this.

**Example:** Someone with income from salary + bonus + freelance has multiple sources with different continuity levels.

**Glossary terms introduced:**
- Income Structure
- Income Source
- Income Pattern
- Income Dependency

---

### Level 3: Analysis Concept

**Core Realization:** "You can evaluate structure systematically"

**Where introduced:**
- How It Works (methodology explanation)
- Glossary: Structural Analysis pages
- Use case pages (applied analysis)

**Simple explanation:** We look at three dimensions of structure: concentration (how dependent on few sources?), continuity (how stable are the sources?), visibility (can we see forward?).

**Example:** Concentration risk: 60% of income from one client. Continuity risk: all contracts end this year. Visibility: unclear if replacement income will appear.

**Glossary terms introduced:**
- Income Concentration
- Source Diversity
- Continuity Strength
- Forward Visibility
- Structural Risk

---

### Level 4: Methodology Concept

**Core Realization:** "Our methodology is fixed, repeatable, and version-stamped"

**Where introduced:**
- How It Works (methodology detail)
- Methodology page (comprehensive explanation)
- Advisor/Organization pages (professional context)

**Simple explanation:** We use the same methodology every time. Same inputs produce the same result. We version-stamp every output so you know what methodology was used.

**Example:** Your 2026 assessment uses Methodology v1.3. If you reassess next year using v1.3, you're using the same framework. If we update to v1.4, we'll show you what changed.

**Glossary terms introduced:**
- Deterministic Result
- Version-Stamped Output
- Structural Verification
- Methodology Version
- Repeatable Framework

---

### Level 5: Classification Concept

**Core Realization:** "Based on structure, we can classify stability"

**Where introduced:**
- Results page (after verification)
- Glossary: Stability Classification page
- Use case pages (classification applied)

**Simple explanation:** Based on concentration, continuity, and visibility, we classify your income stability as a Stability Category. This tells you how much structural confidence you can have.

**Example:** "Your income structure shows High Concentration (risky), Strong Continuity (good), Limited Visibility (uncertain). Overall Stability Classification: Moderate, with concentration as primary concern."

**Glossary terms introduced:**
- Stability Classification
- Income Stability Score™
- Structural Confidence
- Stability Category
- Primary Concern

---

### Level 6: Application Concept

**Core Realization:** "Your structural classification affects your financial decisions"

**Where introduced:**
- Use case pages (decision application)
- Results interpretation page
- Advisor/Organization pages (decision support)

**Simple explanation:** Understanding your income structure helps you make better financial decisions. If you have high concentration, that affects mortgage qualification, business expansion timing, career transition, or investment decisions.

**Example:** "Your concentration risk means lenders may require a larger deposit. Your continuity strength means they'll trust income projection. Your visibility gap means you may need additional documentation."

**Glossary terms introduced:**
- Structural Confidence in Decision
- Decision-Specific Risk
- Financial Decision Support
- Structural Clarity
- Confidence in Decision Outcome

---

### Level 7: Strategic Concept

**Core Realization:** "You can make structural improvements"

**Where introduced:**
- Strategic insights (may be in results or future planning page)
- Advisor/Consultant guidance (future content)

**Simple explanation:** Understanding your structural weaknesses suggests improvements. High concentration? Diversify income. Low visibility? Build more stable revenue. Strong continuity? Leverage this strength.

Verification is not a simple "upgrade funnel." It's a trust-building progression from free transparency to full structural insight.

### Stage 1: Education (Free)

**What user sees:**
- Explanation of what structural verification means
- Factors we evaluate (concentration, continuity, forward visibility)
- What they'll learn about their income
- Privacy/data handling

**Goal:** User understands what they'll get before providing data

**Pages involved:**
- Use case page (relevance to their decision)
- How It Works (methodology overview)
- Free verification page (what they'll see)

---

### Stage 2: Input (Free)

**What user does:**
- Provides basic income information
- Explains income sources
- Describes income patterns
- Takes ~2 minutes

**What happens in background:**
- System maps income structure
- Identifies concentration, continuity, visibility factors
- Prepares analysis

**Goal:** User feels they've provided enough information for insight

**Pages involved:**
- Free verification intake page
- Progress indicators (showing what's being analyzed)

---

**What user sees:**
- High-level structural analysis

- Detailed reports

## UPGRADE ARCHITECTURE (Non-Aggressive)

Upgrade moments are not "sales funnels." They're "decision support opportunities." Users upgrade when they need deeper confidence in their decision, not because of pressure.

### Upgrade Trigger 1: Complexity

**When:** User's income structure is complex (multiple sources, variable patterns)

**CTA language:** "Your income structure is complex. Full structural analysis helps you understand all factors."

**Not:** "Unlock full insights!" "See everything we know!" "Get premium access!"
