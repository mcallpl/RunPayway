# RunPayway™ Page Blueprints

**Decision-State Oriented Page Architecture**

Pages are not just section sequences. Each page serves a specific user decision state, trust requirement, and institutional purpose.

---

## BLUEPRINT FRAMEWORK

Each blueprint defines:
- **User mindset** — Why did they arrive?
- **Trust requirement** — How much institutional explanation do they need?
- **Page purpose** — What decision are they trying to make?
- **Preferred section rhythm** — How should content flow?
- **Content density** — Deep analysis vs. quick clarity?
- **CTA posture** — Action-oriented or exploratory?
- **SEO/entity role** — How does this page own semantic territory?
- **Allowed components** — System components only
- **Forbidden patterns** — No gradients, glow, floating cards, etc.
- **Mobile behavior** — Responsive reflow at 768px/1024px

---

## 1. TRUST ESTABLISHMENT BLUEPRINT

**Use case:** How It Works, Methodology, Standards, Institutional Authority

**User mindset:**
- Skeptical or unfamiliar with RunPayway™
- Wants to understand methodology before deciding
- Values institutional credibility over marketing speed
- Seeking objective explanation, not sales pitch

**Trust requirement:** HIGH
- Clear methodology explanation
- No hand-waving or vague claims
- Fixed, deterministic system
- Version-stamped results philosophy
- Structural clarity

**Page purpose:**
- Establish that RunPayway™ is a framework, not a product
- Explain the fixed methodology
- Demonstrate structural rigor
- Build confidence in deterministic results

**Preferred section rhythm:**
1. Hero (headline, subheadline, supporting text)
2. "The Methodology" section (what we evaluate)
3. "The Framework" section (how it works)
4. "The Results" section (what you get)
5. CTA section (Next steps)
6. Optional: Comparison or deeper methodology section

**Content density:** HIGH
- Detailed explanation preferred
- Structural precision over brevity
- Technical clarity without jargon

**CTA posture:** Exploratory
- "Learn More"
- "See Results"
- "Schedule a Review"
- NOT aggressive urgency

**SEO/entity role:**
- Owns semantic territory: "structural verification," "fixed methodology," "deterministic results"
- Internal linking hub to deeper methodology pages
- Authority page for industry/AI search understanding

**Allowed components:**
- PageHero, SectionContainer, SectionLabel
- TextBlock, PrimaryButton, InstitutionalDivider
- Callout boxes (within system tokens)
- Simple comparison tables

**Forbidden patterns:**
- No animated explainers
- No carousel features
- No testimonials on methodology pages
- No "Why We're Different" vs. competitor language
- No hype language or transformation narratives

**Mobile behavior:**
- Single column at 768px
- Sections stack naturally
- CTA buttons full-width on mobile

## 2. CONCEPT CLARIFICATION BLUEPRINT

**Use case:** /learn/* pages, Glossary, Income Stability Definitions, Structural Concepts

**User mindset:**
- Seeking specific knowledge
- May not yet know what they don't know
- Values clear definitions and examples
- Learning mode, not decision mode (yet)

**Trust requirement:** MEDIUM
- Clear, canonical definitions
- Examples that feel real
- Structured progression from simple to complex
- Internal linking to related concepts

**Page purpose:**
- Define institutional vocabulary
- Build semantic authority
- Support internal linking strategy
- Serve AI search understanding

**Preferred section rhythm:**
1. Hero (simple definition)
2. Core concept section
3. Why this matters section
4. Examples or use cases section
5. Related concepts (internal links)
6. Optional: Deep dive section
7. Optional: FAQ section

**Content density:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- Explanation-forward, not sales-forward
- Structured progression
- Examples help clarify

**CTA posture:** Exploratory
- "Explore Related Concepts"
- "See Practical Example"
- "Read Next"
- Soft, not pushy

**SEO/entity role:**
- Owns semantic authority for specific terms
- Anchor pages for internal linking clusters
- Topic authority signals for AI search

**Allowed components:**
- PageHero, SectionContainer, SectionLabel
- TextBlock, InstitutionalDivider
- Definition callout boxes
- Simple inline examples
- Related links sections

**Forbidden patterns:**
- No carousel features
- No testimonial sections
- No product pitches
- No aggressive CTAs
- No visual fluff or decorative elements

**Mobile behavior:**
- Single column at 768px
- Definition boxes remain readable
- Links stack naturally

## 3. DECISION SUPPORT BLUEPRINT

**Use case:** Use cases (Mortgage qualification, Career transitions, Self-employment, Business expansion, Retirement planning, Investment decisions)

**User mindset:**
- Facing a specific financial decision
- Wants to understand how RunPayway™ helps with THEIR situation
- Values relevance and practical application
- May be comparing to other solutions

**Trust requirement:** HIGH
- Specific methodology application
- Real-world structure explanation
- Transparent about what we can/cannot evaluate
- Risk/limitation clarity

**Page purpose:**
- Show how RunPayway™ methodology applies to a specific decision context
- Build confidence that we understand their structural situation
- Support decision-making process
- Move toward assessment or advisory engagement

**Preferred section rhythm:**
1. Hero (decision context specific)
2. "The Decision" section (what decision are they making?)
3. "The Structural Challenge" section (what makes this hard to evaluate?)
4. "How Structural Verification Helps" section (methodology application)
5. "The Process" section (what happens next?)
6. CTA section (Get Assessment)
7. Optional: Limitations/transparency section

**Content density:** MEDIUM
- Focused on application, not deep theory
- Practical structure explanation
- Clear next steps

**CTA posture:** Action-oriented
- "Get Structural Assessment"
- "Schedule Review"
- "Begin Analysis"
- More assertive than exploratory

**SEO/entity role:**
- Owns semantic territory for decision types
- Supports internal linking from use case clusters
- Differentiates by showing methodology application depth

**Allowed components:**
- PageHero, SectionContainer, SectionLabel
- TextBlock, PrimaryButton, InstitutionalDivider
- Process step boxes (using system tokens)
- Simple comparison tables
- Timeline/process diagrams (text-based, not animated)

**Forbidden patterns:**
- No testimonials (use case authenticity through methodology clarity, not social proof)
- No "success stories" framing
- No competitor comparisons
- No transformation narratives

**Mobile behavior:**
- Single column at 768px
- Process steps stack naturally
- CTA remains prominent

## 4. VERIFICATION ENVIRONMENT BLUEPRINT

**Use case:** Individual, Advisor, Organization environments/pathways

**User mindset:**
- Role-specific (Individual, Advisor, Organization admin)
- Seeking environment-specific functionality
- Wants clear role capabilities and process
- Decision: Should I engage with this role/pricing tier?

**Trust requirement:** MEDIUM
- Clear role definition
- Transparent process and timeline
- Pricing/value clarity
- Capability scope clarity

**Page purpose:**
- Clarify role-specific value
- Explain process and workflow
- Build confidence in role fit
- Move toward engagement/signup

**Preferred section rhythm:**
1. Hero (role-specific value prop)
2. "This is for you if..." section (role definition)
3. "The Process" section (workflow clarity)
4. "What You Get" section (role-specific value)
5. Pricing/licensing section (if applicable)
6. CTA section (Get Started)
7. Optional: FAQ section (role-specific questions)

**Content density:** MEDIUM
- Process clarity essential
- Value specificity important
- Avoid complexity

**CTA posture:** Action-oriented
- "Get Started"
- "Request Access"
- "Learn About Pricing"
- Clearer signal to engage

**SEO/entity role:**
- Role-specific landing pages
- Supports customer journey segmentation
- Clear entity role schema (Individual, Advisor, Organization)

**Allowed components:**
- PageHero, SectionContainer, SectionLabel
- TextBlock, PrimaryButton, InstitutionalDivider
- Capability checkboxes (using system tokens)
- Simple pricing tables
- Process step boxes

**Forbidden patterns:**
- No feature card carousels
- No "sign up now" urgency language
- No testimonial overload
- No "limited time" language

**Mobile behavior:**
- Single column at 768px
- Pricing tables scroll horizontally if needed
- CTA fixed or very prominent

## 5. STRUCTURAL COMPARISON BLUEPRINT

**Use case:** Income structure comparison, Stability profiles, Industry comparisons, Risk pattern pages

**User mindset:**
- Evaluating alternatives or understanding differences
- Wants clear, objective comparison
- May not know what to compare yet
- Values structural clarity, not persuasion

**Trust requirement:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- Objective comparison criteria
- Transparent assumptions
- No obvious bias toward one option
- Methodology clarity

**Page purpose:**
- Clarify structural differences between options
- Support decision-making through understanding
- Own semantic territory for comparison types
- Demonstrate structural rigor

**Preferred section rhythm:**
1. Hero (what are we comparing?)
2. "The Options" section (define alternatives)
3. "Structural Differences" section (comparison framework)
4. Detailed comparison section (side-by-side table or text)
5. "What This Means" section (interpretation guide)
6. CTA section (Explore Assessment)
7. Optional: Deep methodology section

**Content density:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- Comparison accuracy essential
- Structural clarity required
- Interpretation guidance helpful

**CTA posture:** Exploratory to Action
- "Explore Options"
- "See Assessment"
- "Get Structural Analysis"
- Tone: understanding supports decision-making

**SEO/entity role:**
- Owns comparison semantics
- Supports informed decision-making signals
- Authority through objective comparison

**Allowed components:**
- PageHero, SectionContainer, SectionLabel
- TextBlock, PrimaryButton, InstitutionalDivider
- Comparison tables (structured, not flashy)
- Callout boxes for interpretation
- Internal linking to related concepts

**Forbidden patterns:**
- No "vs." language (avoid competitive framing)
- No recommendation bias
- No motivational language
- No feature icons or visual fluff
- No animated comparisons

**Mobile behavior:**
- Comparison tables scroll horizontally
- Single column at 768px
- Readability priority over visual compression

## BLUEPRINT SELECTION GUIDE

**Ask these questions when planning a new page:**

1. **Why is the user arriving?** → Narrows to 2-3 blueprints
2. **What decision are they facing?** → Further clarifies
3. **How much institutional explanation do they need?** → Determines content density
4. **What is the next action we want them to take?** → Determines CTA posture

**Example:**

- User: "Why should I use RunPayway™ for my mortgage decision?"
- Arrives at: Decision Support Blueprint
- Content density: Medium (focus on application, not deep theory)
- CTA: "Get Structural Assessment" (action-oriented)
- Components: Trust-building process explanation, structured workflow, clear next step
