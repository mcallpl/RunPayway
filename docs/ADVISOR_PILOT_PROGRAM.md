# Advisor Pilot Program

**Owner**: CTO / Business Development  
**Effective Date**: Week 4 (after Tier 1 complete)  
**Duration**: 8 weeks  
**Target**: 10 advisors, measurable adoption & feedback  
**Status**: READY (execution begins after Tier 1)

---

## Core Principle

**Customer evidence is created, not discovered.**

You don't get 10 advisors by waiting. You recruit them.

This program is how you systematically generate the market evidence that will shape RunPayway's next year.

---

## Program Structure

### Cohort: 10 Advisors

**Profile**:
- Independent or small firm (1-10 advisors)
- Active in financial advisory / planning
- Willing to use new tools and provide feedback
- Representative of target market (not unusual outliers)

**Recruitment**:
- Outreach: LinkedIn, direct email, industry networks
- Pitch: "Help shape the future of income stability assessment"
- Incentive: Free first 3 months, feedback sessions count as learning
- Selection: Diverse industries (insurance, advisory, accounting, wealth management)

**Timeline**: Weeks 1-3 (after Tier 1 complete)

---

## Program Phases

### Phase 1: Onboarding (Week 1)

**Goal**: Get advisors to first assessment

**Activities**:
- 1-on-1 onboarding call (30 min each)
- Walk through platform basics
- Explain RunPayway's purpose (income stability assessment)
- Set expectations (this is a pilot, feedback is critical)
- Answer questions

**Success Metric**: 10/10 advisors complete onboarding

---

### Phase 2: First Assessment (Week 2)

**Goal**: Prove platform works end-to-end

**Activities**:
- Advisors create 1-2 assessments on real/test clients
- Use RunPayway's assessment tool
- Generate a report
- Provide immediate feedback (what worked? what confused you?)

**Success Metrics**:
- 9+/10 advisors complete first assessment
- <5% abandon during assessment
- <5 minute average assessment time
- All assessments complete without error

**If failures occur**: 
- Immediate troubleshooting
- Document issue
- Push fix if critical

---

### Phase 3: Real Usage (Weeks 3-5)

**Goal**: Advisors integrate into real workflow

**Activities**:
- Advisors use RunPayway with real clients
- Create 5-10 assessments per advisor
- Integrate into their advisory process
- Weekly check-in calls (30 min)

**Check-in Questions**:
- How many assessments did you create this week?
- Did clients find value in the results?
- What would make this more useful?
- Would you recommend this to a colleague?

**Success Metrics**:
- 8+/10 advisors stay active
- 5+ assessments per advisor
- >50% advisors recommend to peer
- >80% advisors plan to continue after pilot

---

### Phase 4: Feedback & Iteration (Weeks 6-7)

**Goal**: Identify what works, what needs fixing

**Activities**:
- Structured feedback survey (10 questions)
- Group feedback call (90 min, all 10 advisors)
- Identify patterns (what's working universally? what's broken?)
- Prioritize fixes for Tier 2

**Survey Topics**:
- Ease of use (1-10 scale)
- Value to clients (1-10 scale)
- Trust in accuracy (1-10 scale)
- Likelihood to recommend (1-10 scale)
- Top 3 improvements needed
- Would you pay for this?

**Group Call**:
- Share common feedback
- Discuss features advisors want
- Discuss pricing ideas
- Talk about institutional features

---

### Phase 5: Measurement & Decision (Week 8)

**Goal**: Decide on next steps based on evidence

**Analysis**:
- Calculate customer evidence metrics (see below)
- Assess program success
- Determine product direction

**Decision Options**:
- ✅ **Continue (market validates)**: Evidence is strong, expand pilot or move to beta
- ⚠️ **Iterate (need improvements)**: Evidence is mixed, address feedback, run second pilot
- ❌ **Pivot (market rejects)**: Evidence is weak, reconsider positioning or target market

## Success Criteria (End of Week 8)

### Minimum Success

**Pilot is successful if**:
- ✅ 8+ advisors stay engaged (80%+ retention)
- ✅ 50+ assessments created (5+ per advisor)
- ✅ >90% completion rate (low UX friction)
- ✅ 7+/10 satisfaction (advisors see value)
- ✅ 3+ would recommend (word-of-mouth seeds)

**Decision**: Move to Tier 2, expand pilot to 30 advisors

---

### Strong Success

**Pilot is strong if**:
- ✅ 9+ advisors stay engaged (90%+ retention)
- ✅ 75+ assessments created (7.5+ per advisor)
- ✅ >95% completion rate (excellent UX)
- ✅ 8+/10 satisfaction (strong product-market fit signal)
- ✅ 5+ would recommend (clear referral loop)
- ✅ 7+ would pay (monetization proven)

**Decision**: Accelerate growth, begin enterprise conversations

---

### Weak Success / Concerns

**Pilot shows issues if**:
- ❌ <7 advisors engaged (70%+ churn)
- ❌ <40 assessments (advisors not using)
- ❌ <85% completion rate (UX problems)
- ❌ <6/10 satisfaction (product doesn't resonate)
- ❌ <2 would recommend (no word-of-mouth)

**Decision**: Iterate on product, address feedback, run second pilot

## Advisor Communication Plan

### Recruitment Email

```
Subject: Help Shape the Future of Income Stability Assessment

Hi [Advisor Name],

I'm building RunPayway, a tool that helps advisors assess and communicate income stability to clients.

I'm recruiting 10 advisors for an 8-week pilot program where you'll:
- Use RunPayway with your real clients (free for the pilot)
- Get detailed feedback from me on how it works
- Help shape the direction of the product

In return, I need:
- 5-10 assessments using your real client data
- Weekly 30-minute feedback calls
- Honest feedback on what works and what doesn't

This is an early-stage product. If you're comfortable with that, I'd love to have you in the pilot.

Are you interested? If so, let's set up a 15-minute call this week.

Best,
CJ
```

```
1. Introductions (5 min)
   - Who is CJ? Why RunPayway?

2. Product Overview (10 min)
```
