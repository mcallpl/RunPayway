**Content:**

---

### THE PRIMARY FINDING

**Your Income Structure Is Defined By:** Fragile Income Concentration

---

### WHAT THIS MEANS

92% of your income comes from a single client relationship.

58% of that income is discretionary (performance-dependent, not guaranteed).

92% of your income requires your active participation.

These three characteristics create a structure with a single point of failure.

If that primary client relationship ends, your income does not gradually decline. It collapses.

This is not a statement about your earning ability. This is a structural fact about how your income is organized.

---

### WHY THIS IS THE PRIMARY CHARACTERISTIC

Your income structure would be described differently if you had:
- Multiple clients (concentration reduces vulnerability)
- Recurring contracts (reduces discretionary portion)
- Passive income components (reduces labor dependence)
- A business that runs without you (reduces labor dependence)

You have none of these. Instead, you have the inverse on all dimensions.

This concentration is not incidental. It is the defining feature. Everything else flows from it.

---

### SUPPORTING CHARACTERISTICS

**Discretionary Income Dominance**
More than half your income depends on business performance, not recurring contracts. This affects your capacity to commit to fixed obligations.

In a weak year, this portion shrinks. In a strong year, it expands. But the weak-year scenario is what matters for a 30-year commitment.

**Labor-Dependent Income**
Your income requires you. You scale only through your own effort. You cannot delegate it, automate it, or hand it off.

This limits your optionality. If you need to step back, your income steps back with you.

---

### HOW THESE WORK TOGETHER

Concentration without discretionary income = vulnerable but manageable

Discretionary income without concentration = risky but diversified

Labor dependence without concentration = scalable through team

Your structure combines all three negatives:
- Concentration (single point of failure)
- Discretionary (dependent on performance)
- Labor-dependent (scales only with your effort)

This combination creates a specific structural vulnerability.

Understanding this vulnerability is the first step toward planning around it.

**Content:**

---

### THE HOME PURCHASE CONTEXT

A home mortgage is a 30-year commitment.

Monthly payments are fixed. Economic cycles vary. Business markets shift.

The structural question: What portion of your income can reliably support that commitment through all three decades, regardless of business performance?

---

### HOW YOUR STRUCTURE ANSWERS THAT QUESTION

**Your guaranteed floor: $92,400/year**

This is the portion that recurs reliably. Even in weak years, this continues.

A 30-year commitment can be supported by income that recurs reliably.

This floor alone can support approximately $360,000 in home value (using standard debt-to-income ratios).

**Your discretionary overlay: $127,600/year**

This is the portion that depends on business performance.

A 30-year commitment cannot be reliably supported by income that depends on annual business results.

Strong years make this portion larger. Weak years make it smaller.

For a commitment that requires certainty, this portion is supplementary, not foundational.

---

### WHAT THIS MEANS STRUCTURALLY

Your $220,000 total income does not support your $220,000 debt-service capacity.

Your income structure reveals a $92,400 guaranteed floor. That is the reliable support.

Your $127,600 discretionary portion is upside in strong years and constraint in weak years.

For a 30-year fixed commitment, you plan around the floor, not the total.

---

### WHY CONCENTRATION MATTERS

If your primary client relationship ends, your income doesn't decline gradually.

Year 1 of mortgage: $220,000
Year 5 of mortgage: $92,400 (if primary client is lost)

That's not a reduction. That's a structural cliff.

Your current commitments can scale with your business. A mortgage cannot. It's fixed.

This is why a $220,000 business owner may encounter constraints that a $120,000 employed earner does not.

It's not about how much you earn. It's about how much is reliable.

---

### THE DECISION IMPLICATION

Understanding your income structure means understanding what you can safely commit to.

Your structure supports a 30-year commitment of approximately $360,000 (based on guaranteed floor alone).

Can you add your discretionary portion for additional capacity? That depends on your risk tolerance and business stability.

But the structural floor is $92,400. That is the reliable foundation.

Planning around this floor is not pessimism. It is structural clarity.

## What Gets Removed (Why These Are NOT in the Report)

### NOT INCLUDED: Advisor Guidance
- "You should diversify"
- "You should build passive income"
- "You should reduce concentration"

**Why:** RunPayway is not an advisor. Advice belongs to other professionals.

### NOT INCLUDED: Lender Policy
- "Lenders will qualify you for X"
- "Lenders require Y debt-to-income ratio"
- "Lenders view business income as Z"

**Why:** RunPayway is not a lender. Policy language weakens the product.

### NOT INCLUDED: Qualification Language
- "You can afford this"
- "You cannot afford that"
- "You qualify for X amount"

**Why:** RunPayway measures structure. Qualification is outside the category.

### NOT INCLUDED: Prediction
- "Your income will likely grow"
- "Markets will probably improve"
- "Your business will likely expand"

**Why:** RunPayway measures what is, not what will be.

### NOT INCLUDED: Generic Financial Advice
- "Emergency funds are important"
- "Diversification reduces risk"
- "You should stress-test your income"

**Why:** This is common knowledge. It doesn't create unique understanding.

### NOT INCLUDED: Multiple Scenarios/Simulations
- "If you lose this client, here's what happens"
- "If markets decline, here's your risk"
- "If you diversify, here's the benefit"

**Why:** This is prediction, not measurement. Framework is frozen.

### NOT INCLUDED: Comparison to Other Business Owners
- "Most business owners have similar concentration"
- "Compared to peers, you're..."
- "Top 20% of business owners..."

**Why:** Benchmarking and comparison are not Decision Check™ functions.

### NOT INCLUDED: Action Plan or Roadmap
- "Step 1: Build passive income"
- "Step 2: Diversify clients"
- "Step 3: Hire a team"

**Why:** RunPayway measures. Implementation is the customer's choice.
