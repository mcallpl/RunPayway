Your income structure reveals three defining characteristics:

Your forward-secured income is 42%. This means 42% of your income is predictable and recurring. The remaining 58% is discretionary—it depends on business performance, market conditions, and your active management.

This is fundamentally different from employment income where forward-secured is typically 90%+ (salary is guaranteed; only bonus is discretionary).

Your structure: 42% guaranteed, 58% discretionary.
Employed earner structure: 90%+ guaranteed, <10% discretionary.

**Characteristic 2: Concentration Creates Structural Vulnerability**

92% of your income is derived from a single business. This means your income structure is singular—it doesn't diversify. If the business changes, your entire income structure changes.

A business owner with 92% concentration has income that moves as one unit. There is no backup. There is no secondary structure.

## DECISION RELEVANCE ANALYSIS™

### For Home Purchase, Your Structural Characteristics Reveal This:

**Structural Reality 1: Your Income Type Creates Lending Classification Challenges**

Home purchase debt service depends on "qualified income." Lenders classify income into categories with different qualification rules:

- **W-2 Employment Income**: Qualifies at 100% (guaranteed)
- **Self-Employment Income**: Qualifies at 50–75% (after business expense deduction and tax adjustment)

Your $220,000 business income does not qualify at 100%. Lenders apply conservative calculations to business income because 58% of it is discretionary and performance-dependent.

**Structural Implication:** Your $220,000 income structurally qualifies as approximately $110,000–$165,000 for debt service purposes. This is not a personal limitation. This is how institutional lending assesses business-dependent income.

---

**Structural Reality 2: Your Forward-Secured Portion Is Your Actual Debt-Service Floor**

Your forward-secured income is 42% ($92,400). This is the portion that is predictable and recurring independent of discretionary performance.

For debt service purposes, your actual financial floor is approximately $92,400, not $220,000.

This matters because:
- Mortgage payment must be supported by income that continues regardless of business performance
- Your guaranteed income floor ($92,400) is what institutional lending focuses on
- Your discretionary income ($127,600) is secondary to debt-service evaluation

**Structural Implication:** Your true debt-service capacity is based on your forward-secured portion, not your total income. A $220,000 business owner may have less debt-service capacity than a $150,000 employed earner (whose $150,000 is 95% forward-secured).

---

**Structural Reality 3: Concentration Creates Institutional Risk**

92% of income from one business creates what institutional lending calls "concentration risk." This means:

- Business loss = income loss
- Business disruption = income disruption
- Business sale = income termination

A diversified income structure (multiple clients, multiple income types, passive income) spreads risk. Your structure concentrates it.

Institutional lenders view 92% concentration as high-risk because income is binary: business succeeds, income continues; business fails, income stops.

**Structural Implication:** Your income structure is viewed as riskier by institutional lenders than an equivalently-earning employed person with diversified income. This affects both qualification and terms.

---

**Structural Reality 4: Your Labor Dependence Creates Structural Fragility**

92% of your income requires your active participation. You cannot extract yourself without losing income.

This is different from business owners who have:
- Passive revenue (doesn't require their labor)
- Management teams (business runs without them)
- Recurring contracts (predictable revenue without active labor)

Your structure means:
- Illness affects income
- Time away affects income
- Business sale affects income continuity
- Forced exit (age, disability) affects income

**Structural Implication:** Your income structure is labor-dependent in a way that creates institutional concern. A lender evaluates your ability to service debt over 30 years. Your income depends on your active participation for 30 years. This is a structural mismatch.
